LUSAIL CORP - LOGO ASSETS (OPTION 2)
Extracted as vectors from Lusail_Corp_Branding.pdf (Option 2: L + C monogram).

BRAND COLORS
  Navy     #030C2B   RGB 3, 12, 43
  Silver   #D0CECC   RGB 208, 206, 204
  Gold     #D3A750   RGB 211, 167, 80
  White    #FFFFFF

FOLDERS
  1-Primary-Logo      Stacked logo: monogram above LUSAIL CORP / HOLDING COMPANY
  2-Icon-Mark         L + C monogram on its own
  3-Wordmark          LUSAIL CORP + HOLDING COMPANY, no monogram
  4-Horizontal-Logo   Monogram beside wordmark, for website headers and letterheads
                      (arranged from the brand parts; not shown in the PDF)
  5-Social-Media      1080 x 1080 profile images (platforms crop them to a circle)
  6-Favicon           Website icons: .ico, .svg, 32 / 192 / 512 px, apple-touch-icon

COLOR VERSIONS
  full-color   Gold L, silver C, navy name, gold tagline, for white backgrounds
  reversed     Gold L, silver C, silver name, gold tagline, for navy or dark backgrounds
               (as shown in the brand PDF)
  white        All white, for photos or single-color dark printing
  navy         All navy, single-color printing on light backgrounds
  black        All black, stamps, newspaper
  gold         (monogram only) for signage, foil or embossing

NOTE
  The silver C is light, so on plain white it has low contrast. On white
  backgrounds where the monogram must read clearly (small sizes, documents),
  the navy version is the safer choice.

FORMATS
  SVG   Vector. Use for print, signage, web. Scales to any size.
  PNG   Transparent background, in three sizes.
