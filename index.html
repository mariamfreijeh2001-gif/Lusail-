The companies behind everyday life in Qatar

Lusail Corp is a Qatari holding company that owns and grows businesses in food, construction, coffee, laundry, energy and trade. Six sectors, one management standard.

Explore our companies Partner with us

    We invest in businesses people rely on every day, and give them the foundation to grow for decades.

    Lusail Corp brings a diverse set of operating companies under one solid structure. Each company keeps its own team and brand, while the group provides capital, governance and shared services. That integration makes each sector stronger and the group more stable.

    What the group gives every company

    A holding company’s job is to make each business stronger than it would be on its own.

    Long-term capital

    Funding for expansion, equipment and new locations, planned in years rather than quarters.

    Governance

    Boards, financial reporting and internal controls held to one standard across every company.

    Shared services

    Group procurement, logistics, IT and HR that lower costs for every sector at once.

    People

    Developing Qatari talent and moving people between companies to build future leaders.
